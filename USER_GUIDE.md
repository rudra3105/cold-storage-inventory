# Daily Use Guide (for staff)

Open the software, sign in, and you land on **Home**. Everything is a big button.

## Every day
| I want to… | Press |
|---|---|
| Record goods that arrived | **Goods In** → choose party → choose product, bags, kg per bag, room/floor/rack → **Save Goods In** → **Print receipt** |
| Record goods that left | **Goods Out** → choose party → choose the stock to send → bags → **Save Goods Out** → **Print receipt** |
| See what is in the warehouse | **Stock** |
| Fix a mistake | Open the entry → **Change** (or **Cancel** and give a reason). Nothing is ever deleted; the **Activity Log** keeps the old version. |

The software will **not** let you send out more than is in stock.

## Money (Owner / Accountant)
- **Make a bill**: Bills → Make a bill → add lines → Save → **Issue**.
- **Money Received**: Record money received → pick customer → amount → (optionally tick which bills it pays).
- **Money Paid**: Record money paid.

## Reports
Reports → choose a report → press a **Quick date** (Today, This month…) → **Print / PDF / Excel**.
Most used: *Current Stock by Party*, *Party Account Statement*, *Goods In Register*, *Goods Out Register*.

## Customers
Each customer gets their own login (Parties → open the party → create login). They see only their own goods, bills and payments, and can **Request Goods**.
When a customer requests goods: **Customer Requests** → Approve → **Create outward** → Save. Stock reduces only then.

## Owner only
**Staff** (add people, change what they can do), **Settings** (business name, address), **Activity Log**.

## First time only
Home shows 3 steps: add storage racks → add products → add your first customer.
